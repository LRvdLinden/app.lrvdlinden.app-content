Simple Chess is the easiest way for children (and grown-ups) to learn chess.

A FRIENDLY COACH
Tap a piece and the coach explains how it moves. Dots show where it can go, and a red ring warns you when one of your pieces is in danger. Stuck? Tap Hint and an arrow shows a good move, with a short explanation of why.

LEARN EVERY PIECE
Short lessons for the pawn, knight, bishop, rook, queen and king, each with a little board to try it out yourself. Special rules like check, castling, promotion and en passant are explained step by step.

PLAY TOGETHER OR AGAINST THE ROBOT
Play with a friend on one iPhone or iPad, or practice against a friendly robot that makes the occasional mistake. Undo a move whenever you like. Your game is saved automatically.

MADE FOR KIDS
• Big, clear pieces and four cheerful board colors
• Works in light and dark mode
• iPhone, iPad and Mac
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
