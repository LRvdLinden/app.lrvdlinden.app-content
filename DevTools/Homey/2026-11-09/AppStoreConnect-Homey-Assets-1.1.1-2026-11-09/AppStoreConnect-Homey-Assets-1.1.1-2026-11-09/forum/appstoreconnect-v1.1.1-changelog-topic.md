# 🚀 App Store Connect v1.1.1 is available

A small polish release on top of 1.1.0. Nothing changes in your devices or Flows: everything you set up keeps working exactly as before.

## ✨ What's new

- **More apps from the developer**: a new card at the bottom of the app settings, in all 13 languages, in light and dark mode and right-to-left for Arabic. It opens lrvdlinden.app.
- **New store description and app text**: a shorter, clearer introduction to the app in the Homey App Store, in all 13 languages.
- **Download charts show one month**: the charts in the widgets now cover the last 31 days instead of roughly two months, so recent trends are easier to read. Totals, last 7 days and last 30 days are unchanged.
- **App icon visible everywhere**: the app icon now uses the standard Homey canvas, and both device types have their icon set explicitly, so they show up correctly in light and dark mode on phone, tablet and web.
- **Support where you'd expect it**: the app's support link and bug reports now point to this forum topic.

## 🔁 Updating

Update from the Homey App Store as usual. Your accounts, API keys, devices, Flows and widgets are kept; no re-pairing needed.

## 📱 Recap of 1.1.0

In case you missed it, 1.1.0 added:

- TestFlight installs and testers per app
- Flow triggers for new TestFlight feedback and crash reports
- Phased release: status, trigger, condition, and pause / resume / release to everyone
- Xcode Cloud: start a build from a Flow and get a trigger when a build finishes
