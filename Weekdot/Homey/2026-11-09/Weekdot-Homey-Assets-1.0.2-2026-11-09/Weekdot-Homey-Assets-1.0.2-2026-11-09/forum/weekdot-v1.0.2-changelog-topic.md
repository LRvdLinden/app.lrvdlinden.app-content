## New version
## 1.0.2

---

### Fixes

🌗 **Widgets in dark mode**: All seven widgets now follow the light or dark mode of your Homey dashboard instead of your phone's setting. Text was unreadable when the two didn't match (dark text on a dark dashboard, or white text on a light one); it is now always readable in both modes.

---

Nothing to set up again: your widgets, Flows and settings keep working after the update.

> **Tip:** Put the **Free or busy** or **Today's timeline** widget on a wall tablet with a dark dashboard; it now stays perfectly readable at night.
