## New version
## 1.0.3

---

### Improvements

🔗 **Connect HealthSync First**: The iPhone connection is now created in the app settings. Go to **Apps → Apple Health → Configure**, tap **Create connection** and paste the Sync URL in HealthSync.

🟢 **Live Connection Status**: The settings page shows the connection status live. "Waiting for HealthSync…" turns into "Connected · your iPhone" as soon as HealthSync tests or syncs the connection.

🛡️ **Pairing Checks the Connection**: Adding the Apple Health device first checks whether HealthSync is connected. If not, pairing explains step by step how to connect HealthSync in the app settings, and no empty device is created. If your iPhone is already connected, pairing continues right away.

⚡ **Data Ready Right Away**: Data that HealthSync sends before the device is added is kept, so the new device is filled immediately after pairing.

📱 **Clear Device Name**: New devices are named after your iPhone, for example "Apple Health (iPhone of Léon)".

🧹 **Clean Removal**: Removing an Apple Health device also removes its connection, once no other device uses it. Create a new connection in the app settings to get a fresh Sync URL.

🌍 **All 13 Homey Languages**: Every new text is available in all Homey languages.

---

Existing Apple Health devices keep working with their current Sync URL. Nothing has to be changed in HealthSync.

> **Tip:** Keep your **Sync URL** private: it contains your personal connection key. If you think it has leaked, remove the device and create a new connection.
