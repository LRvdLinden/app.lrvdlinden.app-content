Privacy Policy – HealthSync

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who we are

HealthSync is an iPhone app made by PixelForges (Léon van der Linden). Contact: homey@vdlinden.xyz.

Summary

HealthSync sends the Apple Health data you choose from your iPhone directly to your own Homey. We, the developer, do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data.

Health data (HealthKit)

The app only reads the HealthKit data types you allow in the iOS permission sheet. It never writes or changes health data. Health data is used only to send it to your own Homey, as you request. It is never used for advertising, marketing, data mining or sold to third parties. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Where your data goes

Data is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection from your iPhone to the private Sync URL of your own Homey, via the Homey cloud connection of Athom B.V. (*.connect.athom.com). There is no server of the developer in between. Processing by Athom is governed by Athom's own privacy policy.

Sync URL

The Sync URL contains a private key. It is stored only in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone. You can create a new one by re-adding the device in Homey.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no tracking of any kind. No account is needed.

Retention and deletion

Your Homey keeps current values, Insights and a local history. Delete the app to remove the Sync URL from your iPhone, and remove the Apple Health device in Homey to delete the data there.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
