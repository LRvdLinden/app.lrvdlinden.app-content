HEALTHKIT CHECKS (common rejection points)
• HealthKit capability ON, NSHealthShareUsageDescription present (read only).
• Do NOT request write access (NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription) — app is read-only.
• Privacy policy URL is mandatory for HealthKit apps and must be reachable.
• Do not use "Apple" in the app name/icon; "Apple Health" may only appear as a
  compatibility reference in description/screenshots (Guideline 5.2.5).
• Optional: use the official "Works with Apple Health" badge only per Apple's
  HealthKit marketing guidelines.

Notes for the reviewer (paste in "Notes"):
-----------------------------------------
HealthSync is a companion app for the Homey smart home hub (by Athom B.V.).
It reads (read-only) HealthKit data the user approves and sends it over HTTPS
directly to the user's own Homey via the Homey cloud connection
(*.connect.athom.com). There is no developer server, no account, no analytics.

How to test without a Homey:
1. Open the app and tap "Authorize Apple Health"; approve some types.
2. Open "All Health Data" to see the values read from HealthKit.
3. Paste this test Sync URL in section 1 and tap "Save & test connection":
   <PASTE A WORKING TEST SYNC URL FROM YOUR OWN HOMEY HERE>
4. Tap "Sync now" — the status shows the result of the sync.
5. Settings -> Auto sync lets the user choose 5–60 minutes (BGAppRefreshTask);
   HealthKit background delivery is used for heart rate, sleep and workouts.
A screen recording of the full flow with a real Homey is attached.

The app does not provide medical advice or diagnosis. It is not affiliated
with Apple Inc. or Athom B.V.
