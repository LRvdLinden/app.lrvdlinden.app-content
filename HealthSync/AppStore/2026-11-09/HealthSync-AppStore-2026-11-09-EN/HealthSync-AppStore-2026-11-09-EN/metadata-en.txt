Description (max 4000)
-----------------------------------------
HealthSync connects the health data on your iPhone to your Homey smart home. Steps, heart rate, sleep, workouts, blood oxygen and more than 180 other data types arrive in Homey as live values, so your home can respond to how you are doing.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Turn on the bedroom lights softly when you wake up
• Start the coffee machine when your sleep is over
• Play a sound when you close all three Activity rings
• Get a notification when your heart rate goes above a value you choose
• Show beautiful health widgets on your Homey dashboard
• Keep history in Homey Insights

HOW IT WORKS
1. Install the free Apple Health app on your Homey and add the device.
2. Copy the private Sync URL from the device settings.
3. Paste it into HealthSync and choose which health data you want to share.
That's it. HealthSync sends new values to your Homey automatically.

AUTOMATIC SYNC
Choose to sync every 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 or 60 minutes. Heart rate, sleep and workouts are also sent as soon as iOS reports new data. iOS decides the exact moment of background syncs, depending on battery and usage.

180+ HEALTH DATA TYPES
Activity, heart, sleep, vitals, body measurements, nutrition, mindfulness, cycle tracking, hearing, mobility and workouts. You decide which types are shared.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Read-only: HealthSync never writes or changes your health data
• Data goes directly from your iPhone to your own Homey over HTTPS
• No developer servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your Sync URL is stored only in the iOS Keychain

ALSO
• Light and dark mode
• Available in 13 languages
• Made by a Homey user, for Homey users

REQUIREMENTS
• A Homey Pro (2019 or later) or Homey Cloud
• The free "Apple Health" app for Homey, available in the Homey App Store
• iPhone with iOS 17 or later
